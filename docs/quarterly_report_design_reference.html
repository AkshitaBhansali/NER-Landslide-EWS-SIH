Appendix

Methodology & KPIs

How risk is scored

Risk is a 0–100 index combining rainfall (live + forecast), soil moisture, pore-water pressure, ground movement and slope susceptibility, weighted by a physics-informed slope-stability model and refined by the ML classifier. Bands: 0–30 low · 30–60 moderate · 60–80 high · 80–100 severe.

KPI definitions

AccuracyTP+TN / total
RecallTP / (TP+FN)
PrecisionTP / (TP+FP)
Sensor uptimeonline / total hrs
